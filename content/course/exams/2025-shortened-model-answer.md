Princess can try collateral estoppel – same issue (whether Darth wrote/used the Tatooine.com malware),

two hours each) they denied a full and fair chance to litigate, and the $1000 AIC provided insufficient
incentive to litigate. The procedures meet all requirements of the Restatement of Conflicts, section 82,

dispute is whether Darth wrote the hacking code, a purely testimonial issue about which the L1 court

evidence needed for both COAs), AUJ renders 1367(b) irrelevant, and facts’ identicality for the two COAs
renders a 1367(c) declination unlikely.
Both the N.D. Fla. (general IPJ & (b)(1),(c)(1) venue because Darth is domiciled in Cerrogordo) and the

assess on R59.
